About Daniel Cunha

Hello! I'm Daniel, a project manager and materials scientist. I spend most of my time coordinating research projects, supporting researchers, and improving how laboratory work is organized. My background in experimental research helps me understand both the technical work and what is needed to keep complex projects moving.

Outside of work, I enjoy building things that help me explore new ideas, usually through coding and design. This site is where I document those projects and what I learn while making them. I value quiet focus over noisy feeds, and wanted a place to share my work beyond the boundaries of my day job.

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you find something here that sparks your interest.
